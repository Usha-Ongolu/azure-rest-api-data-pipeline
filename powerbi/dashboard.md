# Power BI Sales Analytics Dashboard

## Objective

Provide business users with an interactive view of sales
performance.

## KPIs

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Average Order Value

## Visualizations

- Revenue by Country
- Revenue by Product Category
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Top Customers
- Product Performance

## Filters

- Order Date
- Country
- Product Category

## Data Model

The Power BI model follows a star schema:

FactOrder
    |
    +--- DimCustomer
    |
    +--- DimProduct
